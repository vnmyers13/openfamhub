# Release 1.28 — 2026-10-14

**Tag:** `v1.28` (created by `scripts/deploy.sh publish` in CI)
**Images:** `forgejo.vernonmyers.cloud/vernon/openfamhub-{api,web}:1.28` (amd64 + arm64)
**Since:** 1.01 (2026-10-13). Builds 1.02–1.27 were published to `test` one by one; this release collects them.

Release 1.28 adds rewards, badges and a weekly allowance; meal planning with
recipes, a shopping list and a pantry; books, announcements, weather and
birthdays; a dashboard and menu each person can arrange; and offline use for
chores, to-dos and the shopping list. Build 1.08 also replaced the JWT library
and patched the dependencies and base images flagged by the Trivy scan.

## What's new

### Rewards (Phase 6)
- A **Rewards** page where members spend points. A reward can be instant or need an admin's yes; a request takes the points at once, and rejecting or cancelling gives them back.
- **Badges** for chores completed, day streaks or lifetime points, awarded automatically once per person.
- **Weekly allowance** in whole cents, paid Monday 07:00 family time and caught up after downtime (up to 8 weeks). Admins record cash they hand over.
- **Family progress** on the dashboard and the wall: points, this week, streak and badges, updating live.

### Meals (Phase 7)
- A **Meals** page: a weekly plan (breakfast, lunch, dinner, snack), recipes with search, tags, a step-by-step cook mode and video links, a shopping list built from the plan, and a pantry with low-stock and use-by flags.
- **Recipe import** from a link or pasted text, reviewed before it's saved, plus 8 built-in recipes.
- **Fill from a link** in the calendar's New Event form.
- The shopping list resets each Monday: bought items move to history; items you added yourself stay.
- **Scale a recipe** (1.25): change the servings and the ingredient amounts follow, as kitchen fractions.

### Family info and your own layout (Phase 8)
- **Books**: reading lists and a family library; finishing a book earns 50 points, once.
- **Announcements** on the dashboard, with pinning.
- **Weather** from Open-Meteo (no API key). Pick your town under Admin › Settings.
- **Birthdays** on the calendar, the dashboard and the wall, with the age the person is turning.
- **Your own layout**, saved to your account: dashboard pages from templates with cards you add, drag and widen; a gradient or family photo wallpaper; the menu across the top or down the side; "Everyone" or "Just me". All under **My settings** (tap your avatar).

### Offline (Phase 9)
- Ticking and claiming chores, ticking and adding shopping items, and adding and ticking to-dos work without a connection and sync when you're back, even after closing the app.
- An offline banner and "changes waiting" indicator.
- If someone changed the same item meanwhile, you choose: keep mine, keep theirs, or merge.
- The app opens offline with the last copy of chores, to-dos, the shopping list and Home.

### Other
- HEIC/HEIF photo uploads (iPhone photos) and upload progress on the Photos page.
- `scripts/deploy.sh promote <prod-host> <checked-build>` deploys a chosen, already-checked build to production; CI's automatic deploy stays on `test`.
- CI passes the `version` job's release to publish, deploy and Trivy, so `test` can't stay on an older build.

## Security
- **1.08:** `python-jose` replaced with PyJWT (CVE-2026-85394; drops `ecdsa`). Existing sign-ins stay valid. FastAPI/Starlette, python-multipart and Pillow bumped; the web image installs OS updates at build time.
- Every meals endpoint needs a signed-in family member (v0.29's didn't). Recipe and event links are fetched through the SSRF-safe `core/safe_fetch.py`.
- Offline replays carry an Idempotency-Key, so a change is never applied twice.

## Upgrading from 1.01

Back up first ([deployment.md §7](../deployment.md#7-backups-and-restore)). Then upgrade the usual way: CI does it on the `test` VM; elsewhere run `scripts/deploy.sh publish` and `scripts/deploy.sh remote <host>`, or `scripts/deploy.sh update` for a source install.

1. **Migrations 009–011 run at startup** (rewards, meals, books/announcements/birthdays). No configuration changes are needed.
2. **Outbound access:** weather calls `api.open-meteo.com` and `geocoding-api.open-meteo.com`, and recipe/event import fetches the link you give it. If the server's egress is restricted, allow those or weather stays empty.
3. **Set the weather location** under **Admin › Settings** and each member's **birthday** (Admin › Users, or My settings).
4. **Reload wall displays** once (reboot the Pi or refresh) to get family progress and birthdays. Phones may need the app opened twice for the new service worker (offline support).
5. **Developers:** run `npm install` in `frontend/` (adds `fake-indexeddb`, dev only).
