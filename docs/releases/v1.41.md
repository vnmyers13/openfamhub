# Release 1.41 — 2026-10-14

**Tag:** `v1.41` (created by `scripts/deploy.sh publish` in CI)
**Images:** `forgejo.vernonmyers.cloud/vernon/openfamhub-{api,web}:1.41` (amd64 + arm64)
**Since:** 1.28 (2026-10-14). Builds 1.29–1.40 were published to `test` one by one; this release collects them.

Release 1.41 is Phase 10: the app reads photos (a paper shopping list, a recipe
card) on your phone, the wall cycles between the calendar, routines and family
progress, and it can show clocks for other places. It also brings editing
calendar feeds, editing your own profile, the meal plan offline, and fixes to
offline conflicts, rewards and the wall clock.

## What's new

### Reading photos (Phase 10)
- **Scan a list** on the Shopping list: take a photo of a paper list and its items appear with quantities, ready to check and add. Headings and smudges are skipped; things already on the list start unticked.
- **Recipe from a photo:** Recipes › Import › "Or read a photo of it" turns a recipe card or cookbook page into text to check, then reads it like pasted text.
- The photo is read on your phone and never uploaded. The reader is served by OpenFamHub itself, so it works without internet and, after the first scan, offline.

### The wall (Phase 10)
- The right side cycles between the week's calendar, routines and family progress with birthdays, 20 seconds each, with a countdown. Routines is skipped when none is on.
- **Other clocks:** Admin › Settings › Other clocks on the wall adds up to three (say, "Grandma" in London), shown under the wall clock with the day when it differs.
- The wall clock follows the family timezone (it used the display's own zone).
- Paired walls can read today's chores, the week's meals, announcements and the weather (`/api/wall/chores`, `/meals`, `/announcements`, `/weather`), read-only, for the panels that come next.

### Everything else since 1.28
- **Edit calendar feeds** in Admin › Calendars: name, color, sync interval, pause, or a new link (`webcal://` works), which is checked before it's saved.
- **Change your own name, color and avatar** in My settings › Me.
- **The meal plan works offline**, with the same "keep mine / keep theirs / merge" choice as chores, to-dos and shopping.

## Fixed
- Offline "Keep mine" and "Merge" went nowhere: the resolved change reused its Idempotency-Key and got the remembered 409 back. It's now sent as a new request.
- Two rewards redeemed at the same moment (two devices) could spend more points than someone had; the later one is refused.
- The wall clock showed the display's own timezone.

## Security
- Frontend dependencies updated for published advisories: axios 1.20.0, react-router 7.18.4, form-data 4.0.6, moment 2.31.0. `npm audit` finds nothing in what the app ships. Build-only advisories in Tailwind CSS 3's tooling remain (P3; needs Tailwind 4).
- The 44 Debian package findings in the API image are still open (P3): Debian has no fixes yet; every build pulls a fresh base and upgrades.

## Not in this release
- Checking off routine steps from the wall (branch `wall-routine-step-completion`, still to merge).
- Wall panels for chores, meals, announcements and weather (their data endpoints are in; the panels are next).

## Upgrading from 1.28

Back up first ([deployment.md §7](../deployment.md#7-backups-and-restore)). Then upgrade the usual way: CI does it on the `test` VM; elsewhere run `scripts/deploy.sh publish` and `scripts/deploy.sh remote <host>`, or `scripts/deploy.sh update` for a source install.

1. **No migrations and no configuration changes.**
2. **The web image is about 15 MB larger** (the OCR engine and English data under `/ocr/`).
3. **Reload wall displays** once (reboot the Pi or refresh) for the cycling panels and extra clocks.
4. **Phones** pick up the new app the next time it's opened (the service worker may need a second open).
5. **Developers:** `npm install` in `frontend/`; frontend work needs Node 24 (or 22.22+, `frontend/.nvmrc`).
